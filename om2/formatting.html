Formatting Styles and Headings

Formatting Styles

Bold - the b tag makes text bold.

Italics - the i tag makes text slanted.

Underline - the u tag draws a line under text.

Strike - the strike tag draws a line through text.

H2O - the sub tag makes Subscript text (below the line).

X2 - the sup tag makes Superscript text (above the line).
